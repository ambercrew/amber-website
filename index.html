Why Amber

You shouldn't have to study the study app.

Incremental reading is one of the most effective ways to learn, but traditional tools bury it under friction. Amber is built for beginners and experienced incremental learners alike, with a clean, simple design that never trades away the power you need.

Traditional software

  • Overwhelming stats and dashboards

  • Cluttered windows and bloated menus

  • Hours spent learning the software itself

  • One rigid way to study

  • Simpler apps that give up real power

Amber

  • A calm interface built around your reading

  • Only what you need, when you need it

  • Open it and start learning in minutes

  • Keeps your study habits and grows with you

  • Full incremental reading power, no compromises

How incremental learning works in Amber

Start simple. Four steps, a few minutes a day, nothing to configure.

01

Import

Bring in PDFs, ePubs or web content straight from your device.

02

Extract

Highlight the passage that matters. It becomes its own extract, linked to where it came from.

03

Make cards

Turn extracts into cloze deletions with a single tap, or let AI create cards for you.

04

Let it compound

The priority queue decides what you see next, so your knowledge grows naturally over time.

Features

Everything you need. Nothing in your way.

Whether you're importing massive PDFs, creating flashcards, or just building better study habits, Amber adapts to you.

Incremental reading, without the learning curve

Reading, extracts and flashcards live in one prioritized queue, so you always work on what matters most. Keep your existing study habits and move into true incremental reading at your own pace.

100% free & open source

Free in cost and in freedom (MIT licensed). You own your data and can export it anytime.

Effortless importing

Import PDFs, ePubs, web content and more straight from your device in seconds.

Multiplatform ready

Runs on your phone, tablet or computer: Linux, Windows, macOS, iOS and Android.

Cloud sync

Back up your library and sync progress, extracts and cards across devices.

Powered by FSRS

Review less. Remember more.

Amber schedules your cards with FSRS, the modern open-source spaced repetition algorithm. Each card comes back right before you'd forget it, so you spend fewer reviews for the same retention.

Modern scheduling

A smarter successor to the decades-old SM-2 algorithm.

Built around your memory

Intervals adapt to how well you actually remember each card.

Four simple grades

Again, Hard, Good, Easy. That's all you ever press.
